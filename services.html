Trimming & Pruning

Healthy trees start with proper pruning. We remove dead, crossing, and weak limbs to improve structure, reduce wind resistance, and promote long-term health — all while keeping your trees beautiful.

22+ years of hands-on experience means we know how to prune the right way. We never top trees — it's damaging and unnecessary. We explain everything before we cut.

Canopy elevation, crown thinning, deadwood removal

Stump Grinding

Left-behind stumps are trip hazards, lawn mower obstacles, and breeding grounds for insects and fungus. Our stump grinder takes them below grade quickly — leaving a clean surface you can sod, plant, or just forget about.

✓

Ground to 6–12" below surface — ready for sod or seed

✓

All stump sizes, any hardwood species

✓

Chips spread in the hole or hauled off — your choice

✓

Multi-stump discounts available
