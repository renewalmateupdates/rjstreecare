Emergency Situations We Handle

When a tree or limb becomes an immediate threat to your safety, property, or access, it's an emergency. We take these calls seriously, and as a small local crew, we prioritize them above routine work whenever we possibly can.

!

Tree on your house or roof — immediate structural protection + removal

!

Fallen tree blocking road or driveway — cleared same day

!

Hanging or split limbs — over cars, structures, or play areas

!

Leaning tree after storm — at risk of falling on structure or people

!

Root uplift — tree compromised at the base

!

Power line contact — we coordinate with the utility company

⚡ If a tree is touching a power line, stay away and call your utility company first, then call us. We work alongside utility crews to clear the tree safely.

What to Do Right Now

  1. Keep everyone away from the tree
  2. If inside, move to the opposite side of the house from the fallen tree
  3. Do NOT go under hanging limbs
  4. Call us — we handle the rest
