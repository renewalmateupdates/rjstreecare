When Does a Tree Need to Come Down?

Trees are valuable — we don't recommend removal unless it's truly the right call. That said, some situations leave no good alternative:

🪓

Dead or dying trees — structurally compromised and a liability risk

🪓

Disease or severe pest damage — stopping spread to healthy trees

🪓

Storm-damaged trees — split trunks, hanging limbs, root damage

🪓

Too close to structures — threatening your foundation, roof, or utility lines

🪓

Construction or landscaping projects — clearing space for new plans

🪓

Unwanted species — invasive or nuisance trees causing ongoing problems

Not sure if removal is the right answer? We'll come look at the tree and give you an honest assessment — no upsell pressure, no agenda.
