Our Story

Started with a Truck, a Climbing Rig, and a Work Ethic

Ron's been climbing and cutting trees for over 22 years — long before RJ's Tree Care had a name on the truck door. He started small: one truck, basic gear, and a reputation for showing up and doing the job right. Back then, a lot of tree companies in the area were either unreliable or overpriced — sometimes both. Ron figured there was room for something better.

Today, RJ's Tree Care runs trucks, bucket equipment, and an experienced climbing crew across Rushville, Dillsboro, and the surrounding tri-state area — with plans to keep expanding south. The work ethic hasn't changed. Neither has the standard: show up, do it right, and clean up like you were never there.

"We clean up like we wasn't even there!" — Ron
