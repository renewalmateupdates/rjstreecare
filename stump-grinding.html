Why Get Rid of That Stump?

Old stumps are more trouble than they look. They're a tripping hazard, a lawnmower obstacle, an eyesore, and often a home for insects, ants, and fungus that can spread to healthy trees. Grinding takes care of all of it.

✓

Eliminating trip hazards for kids and guests

✓

Stop insects, fungi, and disease from spreading

✓

Reclaim the lawn space — sod or replant right after

✓

Multi-stump discounts available — clear them all at once

✓

Ground 6–12" below surface — deep enough to finish the job

How We Quote Stump Grinding
